# Garden plan 2026

## Tomatoes

Sow tomato seeds indoors on 1 March, six weeks before the last frost, in the
seed trays on the kitchen windowsill. Plant them out after 15 April, once the
nights stay above 10 °C. Varieties this year: Sungold for the pots and San
Marzano for the raised bed.

## Beans

Sow runner beans straight into the ground in the second week of May, along the
fence, with canes 30 cm apart.

## Garlic

Plant garlic cloves in October, 15 cm apart, pointed end up.
